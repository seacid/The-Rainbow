# The Rainbow 「彩虹」

![The Rainbow main interface](assets/Main-UI.png)

[English](README.md) | [简体中文](README_zh.md)

I have always been thinking about the essence of mixing. To me, mixing is about intuitively adding a layer of emotion to audio. These emotions come in many colours, yet few audio plugins seek to express them.

The arrival of the AI agent era has made me wonder whether the time has come to lower the barrier to mixing. Combining different emotions with the most essential elements of mixing, making it accessible to everyone, and helping people quickly find the right processing have always been my goals.

I break mixing down into four core elements: colour, dynamics, emphasis, and space.

![Seven colours and bypass controls](assets/Colors.png)

Looking back at all my past multitrack projects, I have grouped almost every processing chain I use into seven colours. Raise a fader, and you can achieve a pleasing sound. Each colour contains an extremely complex processing chain, including hardware-modelled 2A, 3A, 76, and other compressors, with complex routing designed to let a single fader handle everything. You can blend the colours in different proportions to find the sound that feels right to you. If you are unsure whether a particular colour suits your audio, click the small round button above its fader to bypass that colour. To bypass all colours together, click the group of seven small dots at the top of the plugin.

![THRESHOLD, LEARN, and AUTO](assets/Threshold.png)

The plugin also provides a threshold control to accommodate the dynamics of different audio sources. Play the loudest section of your audio and press LEARN; the plugin will automatically adjust the threshold based on RMS. You can also adjust the threshold manually to control the amount of compression. I strongly recommend enabling automatic gain compensation before using the plugin. Click AUTO in the bottom-right corner to enable it, allowing you to compare changes in the audio’s emotional character at the same RMS level.

![FOCUS, MASK, EQ ADJUST, and SWEEP](assets/EQ.png)

Emphasis is the plugin’s most central concept: you need to tell it what to focus on and what to leave in the background. I have provided two frequency controls, FOCUS and MASK. Drag these faders to audition frequencies, stopping at the frequency ranges you want to highlight or set aside. When they are not needed, click the FOCUS or MASK label to bypass the corresponding frequency processing. When you do not need frequency auditioning, click SWEEP on the right to turn it off, or use the mouse wheel to fine-tune the frequencies. If the EQ processing feels excessive, use EQ ADJUST to adjust its intensity. You can also click EQ ADJUST to bypass all EQ processing.

![SPACE control and bypass](assets/Space.png)

Every colour except red includes spatial effects. The SPACE fader controls the proportion of these effects. Likewise, clicking the SPACE button bypasses all spatial processing.

![MIX and GAIN controls](assets/Mix%26Gain.png)

The plugin also includes a wet/dry control. Turn the MIX knob to adjust the overall processing intensity. A gain control sits at the end of the entire processing chain; turn the GAIN knob to increase the audio level.

![2x option in the More menu](assets/2x-Menu.png)

The plugin supports 2x oversampling. Open the More menu in the upper-right corner and click 2x to enable it.

I hope The Rainbow can be a plugin that is easy to learn, sounds good, is convenient and quick to use, and looks beautiful—one that helps you achieve the result you want without having to think too much, while adding more colour to your audio. This plugin will remain free forever. Thank you for your interest in The Rainbow, and thank you to everyone who contributes to the world of audio.

May we all shine brightly.
